## 📌 Project Overview
This project was developed as part of the premier hackathon organized by the **Data Secrets** community in collaboration with **Dodo Brands**. The challenge focused on leveraging Machine Learning to optimize marketing communication for a global pizza chain.

## 💼 Business Context
**Dodo Brands** is a major international food-tech company operating over 1,000 units across 21 countries. A core part of their retention strategy involves bi-weekly promotional campaigns, offering various incentives such as:
* Percentage-based or fixed-currency discounts.
* "Buy one, get one" (BOGO) offers.
* Gift items with a minimum order value.

**The Problem:** Sending generic promotions to all customers leads to "marketing fatigue," increased costs, and suboptimal conversion rates.
**The Solution:** Developing a predictive model to identify the likelihood of a customer engaging with a specific offer.

## ⚙️ Technical Challenge
The goal was to build a robust binary classification model to predict customer behavior:
* **Target Variable:** Whether a customer will redeem a promo code during its validity period.
* **Objective:** Maximize prediction accuracy to enable hyper-personalized marketing.
* **Impact:** By predicting response probability, the company can optimize its marketing budget and improve the overall customer experience.
